Sprachigo — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Sprachigo ("the App", "we", "us") is a vocabulary and language-learning app for German, English, Turkish, French, Italian, Spanish, Russian, Japanese and Chinese. This policy explains what data the App collects, how it's used, and the choices you have. There is no user account — the App works entirely without you giving us your name or email.

Data stored only on your device

Your learning progress, review schedule, streaks, and app settings (native language, target languages, levels, reminder time) are stored locally on your device using standard on-device storage (SQLite and local preferences). None of this is uploaded to any server we operate — we don't operate a server at all. Uninstalling the App deletes this data.

Advertising

The App shows ads served by Google AdMob to keep it free. AdMob may collect device and advertising identifiers (such as your device's advertising ID), IP address, and general usage data to serve and measure ads, and — where you have not restricted it — to personalize them.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK, or Switzerland, the App shows a consent form (Google's User Messaging Platform) the first time you open it, letting you choose whether ads may use your data for personalization. You can change this choice at any time from Settings → Ad privacy choices inside the App.

See Google's own privacy policy for how it handles this data: policies.google.com/privacy, and its ad-specific policy at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Crash and error reporting

The App may use Sentry to automatically report crashes and errors so we can fix bugs. A crash report can include device model, OS version, app version, and a technical stack trace of where the crash happened — it does not include your name or email, since the App never collects those in the first place.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the App schedules a local notification directly on your device using the OS's own notification system. This never involves sending your data anywhere.

Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ad personalization is automatically disabled for users the App or AdMob identifies as under the applicable age of consent.

Your choices

  • Change or withdraw ad-personalization consent any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • Uninstall the App to remove all locally stored data.
  • Turn off ad personalization at the OS level (iOS: Settings → Privacy → Tracking; Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after a change means you accept the updated policy.
